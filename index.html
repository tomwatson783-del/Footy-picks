Odds & Value Finder

Enter decimal odds from your chosen source. Odds are not fetched automatically. The calculation compares your entered odds with the model's probability for the same 1X2 outcome. It does not guarantee profit and does not account fully for market margin or changing prices.

Historical Model Test

Models are compared on a later chronological holdout sample. Each match is predicted using only earlier completed results available in the retrieved archive.

Higher accuracy is better; lower Brier score and log loss are better. The test sample is not proof of future performance. Public history coverage varies.
